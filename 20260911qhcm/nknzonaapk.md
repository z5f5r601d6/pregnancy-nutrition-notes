6609, cerulean [sɪ'ruːlɪən]

adj. 蔚蓝的，天蓝色的

The makeup artists at Michael Kors, Rebecca Minkoff, Jenny Packham, and Kate Spade channeled spring's blue skies with a dramatic sweep of cerulean shadow.

6610, cervical [sɜːvɪkl; səvaɪkl]

1.ADJ Cervical means relating to the cervix. 子宫颈的

cervical ectropion宫颈柱状上皮异位 （旧译“宫颈糜烂”有误，已废除）

Doctors aim to cut the number of women dying from cervical cancer by half this decade. 

医生们这十年的目标在于使死于宫颈癌的妇女人数减少一半。

Experts say that cervical cancer is preventable and controllable, and the early cure rate can reach more than 90%.专家称宫颈癌可防可控，早期治愈率可达90%以上。

2.ADJ Cervical means relating to the neck. 颈部的

 ...injury to the cervical spine from motor vehicle collisions. …因机动车相撞导致的颈椎受伤。

6611, cervine ['sɜːvaɪn]

adj. 鹿的，像鹿一样的

例句：

The entire horn, or any branch of the horn, of a cervine animal, as of a stag(牡鹿）.

Moose（麋）, as well as other members of the cervine family, live mostly on the shoots of trees, but they die mostly by the shoots of hunters.

6612, cesspit [sespɪt]

( also cess•pool /sespuːl/ )

1.a covered hole or container in the ground for collecting waste from a building, especially from the toilets （覆盖的）粪池，污水坑，垃圾坑

2.a place where dishonest or immoral people gather 藏污纳垢的场所

a cesspit of corruption 堕落腐化的渊薮

6613, CETS [siː iː tiː]

abbr. 大学英语考试（College English Test）；已校正的发动机的测试（Calibrated Engine Testing）；对外统一关税（Common External Tariff）；欧洲中部时间 (Central European Time)

cets (=sets)

（美国俚语）止痛药片
